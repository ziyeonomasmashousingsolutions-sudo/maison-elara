Our Story

Founded on Restraint

Maison Elara began with a simple belief: that true luxury is quiet. Every garment and every piece of jewellery is designed to outlast trend cycles, made in small runs by artisans who take the time craftsmanship deserves.

From our Paris atelier, we source fine wool, silk, and 18k gold vermeil, working with the same small family workshops season after season.
